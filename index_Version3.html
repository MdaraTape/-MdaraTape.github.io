About Me — Building Technology That Matters

I'm Tinerombo T. Mudzimbasekwa, a software developer passionate about building digital solutions that solve real-world problems. My interests span web development, mobile applications, and machine learning, with a focus on turning ideas into practical, user-friendly experiences.
